DevSurf Mobile bundles Peter Lowe's ad and tracking server list unmodified.
Author: Peter Lowe (pgl@yoyo.org)
Homepage: https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/
Source: https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/serverlist.php?hostformat=adblockplus&showintro=1&mimetype=plaintext
Downloaded: 2026-10-17
The list retains the upstream attribution and source headers.
The author permits combining and redistributing the list on the homepage.

DevSurf uses unconditional ||hostname^ rules to block embedded requests to the
listed domains and their subdomains. Main-frame navigation remains allowed.
The bundled list is refreshed with app releases; no runtime network updates
or external browsing-data reporting are performed. DevSurf's small ad-slot
stylesheet is application-owned and is not copied from a filter subscription.
